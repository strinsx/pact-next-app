# Pact — Brand & Content Guide

## Introduction

**Pact** is a group accountability platform designed to help people stay consistent with their goals through shared commitment and social accountability.

Unlike traditional to-do lists or task management applications, Pact focuses on making a daily commitment and following through on it. Users can create personal commitments, submit their progress before a scheduled evaluation time, and allow their accountability groups to review, encourage, and keep them accountable.

The philosophy behind Pact is simple:

> **Make a commitment. Keep your word. Build consistency.**

---

# What Pact Solves

Many people know what they should do but struggle to remain consistent.

Pact solves this by introducing accountability.

Instead of working completely alone, users make a public commitment that is visible to their accountability groups. At the end of the day, they report whether they completed it, creating a simple but effective accountability loop.

# Core Values

- Accountability over productivity
- Consistency over perfection
- Progress over motivation
- Community over isolation
- Honesty over appearances

---

# Brand Personality

Pact should feel:

- Professional
- Encouraging
- Clean
- Focused
- Trustworthy
- Minimal
- Motivational without being overwhelming

The application should avoid looking like a social media platform or a gamified productivity app. It should instead feel calm and intentional.

---

# Brand Voice

The tone of the application should be:

- Direct
- Friendly
- Encouraging
- Confident
- Clear

Avoid:

- Excessive hype
- Corporate jargon
- Negative language
- Guilt-inducing messages

Example:

❌ "You failed today's commitment."

✅ "Today's commitment was missed. Tomorrow is another opportunity."

Instead of:

```

```

```

```

or


# Overall Brand Philosophy

Pact is not a task manager.

Pact is not a habit tracker.

Pact is not a social media platform.

Pact is an accountability platform where people build consistency by making commitments and following through on them together.

Every feature, interaction, and piece of copy should reinforce one central idea:

> **Make a commitment. Keep your word. Build consistency.**
